Mastery costs

What it takes to finish each mastery you have left: the fastest plan to reach Master (10,000), Grandmaster (100,000) and Megamaster (1,000,000) from your current progress, and what that plan spends in gold. Each row is cumulative — a Grandmaster row includes the work Master needed. Everything is read from the masteries, inventory and settings you share with the solver page — edit any of them under ⚙ Settings, top right; times assume the steady-state (yearly-average) calendar. Click a row for that mastery's full plan, or a column's sort button to reorder the table.
