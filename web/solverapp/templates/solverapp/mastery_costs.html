Mastery costs

What it takes to reach the next tier of each mastery you have left: the fastest plan to Master (10,000), Grandmaster (100,000) or Megamaster (1,000,000) — whichever is next — from your current progress, and what that plan spends in gold. Everything is read from the masteries, inventory and settings you share with the solver page — edit any of them under ⚙ Settings, top right; times assume the steady-state (yearly-average) calendar. Click a row for that mastery's full plan, or a column's sort button to reorder the table; the ▸ beside an item shows its tiers after the next one, worked out the first time you open it.
